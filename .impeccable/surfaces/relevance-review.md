# Local relevance review

Mode: Operate. This extends AgentForge's existing field-guide design into a
private evaluation worksheet. It is not a public website route.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Review one ordinary task against the complete pinned catalog. Keep
expected answers and retrieval rankings outside the worksheet.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit warm paper #f8f5ed, reading white #fffdf8, forest ink #243c31,
muted olive #586355 and fine parchment rules #dad6c7. Use Geist for controls and
data. Flat rows and familiar form controls carry the work.

STORY: Read or revise a task, decide whether a useful capability exists, inspect
source instructions, record useful identities and a reason, then continue.
Progress stays in this browser and exports to a local review file.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact title and progress bar precede two work areas. The
task and judgment form occupy the left column; searchable catalog rows occupy
the wider right column. On a phone the task precedes the catalog. The primary
action marks the task reviewed and advances; saving a draft remains visible.

FORM: Code-led extension with a fixed task-and-catalog worksheet composition.
No new visual identity or open concept selection. Expand a row to inspect
pinned source text without losing the task.

BEHAVIOR: On desktop, the sticky task pane has its own vertical scroll within
the viewport so the judgment and review action remain reachable while browsing
the catalog. At 760px and below, the task returns to normal page flow before
the catalog. An empty feedback live region takes no layout space; messages
appear in the header when present.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Only the synthetic demo is available to visual reviewers. The real held-out
tasks remain outside their context. This surface creates no new design tokens.
