## What this suggests changing next

1. Match the requested operation and object. A request to analyze existing transcripts should not become transcription; a pricing-page request should not become cloud billing.
2. Avoid filling a shortlist with weak alternatives merely because one result fits.
3. Show assumptions such as Python, PostgreSQL or Notion before treating a skill as ready for the task.
4. Group purpose-equivalent variants in a shortlist without deleting distinct upstream packages or hiding provenance.

Live catalog inspection also found `competitive-landscape` and frontend design review capabilities. Their presence is evidence of possible ranking misses, not proof that either is the best answer for a task. Broader research coverage still needs review.
